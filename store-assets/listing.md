## Full description (Google Play; also use as the App Store description)

```text
Kadam helps you build good habits one step at a time. Check off your habits each day, watch your streaks grow, and see your progress over the weeks. Everything stays on your phone.

SIMPLE DAILY TRACKING
• Tap to check off a habit, or swipe right to complete and left to skip
• Yes/no habits, or measurable goals like 8 glasses of water or 20 pages
• Every day, chosen weekdays, or a number of times per week
• Add a short note to any day, and fill in days you forgot to log

STREAKS AND STATS
• Current and best streak for every habit, following its own schedule (a Mon/Wed/Fri habit isn't broken on Tuesday)
• A monthly calendar for each habit
• Weekly charts and completion rates for the last 7, 30 or 90 days

GENTLE REMINDERS
• A reminder at the time you choose for each habit
• An optional evening check-in if something is still open
• No reminder for habits you've already done or skipped that day

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Works offline, with no account or sign-in
• Your data is stored only on your phone and is never sent anywhere
• No ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Back up everything to one file and restore it on a new phone, or export your history as CSV

COMFORTABLE TO USE
• Light and dark themes, or follow your phone's setting
• Undo for accidental taps
• Works with screen readers, large text and reduced motion

"Kadam" means "step". Small steps, repeated every day, add up.
```

## Category

**Recommendation: Productivity** (Google Play), with **Productivity** as the primary and **Health & Fitness** as the secondary category on the App Store.

Kadam tracks any kind of habit (reading, journaling, chores as well as water or exercise) and gives no health advice, so Productivity describes it best and sets the right expectations. Health & Fitness is a reasonable second choice on the App Store, where a secondary category is allowed; on Google Play only one category can be picked.
