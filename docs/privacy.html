Privacy Policy for Kadam: Habit Tracker

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Kadam is a habit tracker that keeps everything on your phone. This policy explains, in plain language, what that means.

The short version

  • Your data stays on your device. Kadam has no servers.
  • We don’t collect, share, sell or send any of your information anywhere.
  • There’s no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What Kadam stores, and where

Kadam stores the habits you create, the days you log, your notes and your settings (such as theme and reminder times). All of it is saved only on your device, in the app’s private storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and the app doesn’t connect to the internet to work.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications: they are scheduled by the app on your device. No push service or server is involved. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or in your phone’s settings at any time.

Backups and exports

When you use Back up data or Export data as CSV, Kadam creates a file and hands it to your phone’s share sheet. You decide where it goes (for example, your files, a cloud drive or an email), and that service’s own privacy terms apply. When you restore a backup or import a CSV, Kadam reads only the file you pick.

Your phone’s own system backup (such as iCloud Backup on iPhone or Google backup on Android) may include Kadam’s data, depending on your phone’s settings. Those backups belong to your Apple or Google account, not to us.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything at any time with Settings → Reset all data. Deleting the app also deletes its data from your device. Any backup files you saved yourself, and your phone’s own system backups, are kept until you delete them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set. Optional.
  • Vibration: for small haptic feedback when you check off a habit.

Kadam doesn’t access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or any other personal data.

Children

Kadam doesn’t collect any personal information from anyone, including children.
